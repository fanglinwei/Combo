# Combo 引导设计稿

Scope: `docs/design/liquid-glass/sources/combo-onboarding-liquid-glass.html` and its preview copies. Mode: Operate. Extend the established Liquid Glass world; code-led, no approved image comp. Keep native App implementation and unrelated working changes untouched.

Audience and job: A new Combo user on a Mac, in either system appearance, needs to find the menu-bar icon, open and close the panel, and finish the guide. Preserve the existing help, hardware-dependent battery copy, menu-bar example, live panel visibility feedback, and direct completion. The guide never blocks completion on trying the panel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Teach the actual menu-bar interaction in one calm welcome window. Recognition, trying the panel, and completion form a progressive flow without a multi-page setup ritual.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the fusion panel's cool neutral glass, graphite/silver primary action, system typography, and colored SF Symbols. The welcome window is the structural layer; the menu-bar callout and the anchored panel are lighter functional layers. Follow macOS light/dark and accessibility preferences.

STORY: Find Combo at the upper-right menu bar, click to open, click again to close, then choose 开始使用. Keep Command-drag help and 设置 → 关于 guidance findable. Demonstration values are labeled as examples.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A menu bar across the preview with a pointed callout on the real demonstration trigger. Beneath it, a compact native welcome window: brand and title, concise value statement, a menu-bar example, live instruction, help disclosure, and one primary button at the lower-right. The panel opens from the same trigger and closes along the same path; completion dismisses the welcome window.

FORM: One-page native welcome with progressive interaction states, first choice for the existing single-action job. User-pinned existing world and preserved function; no concept roll. seed: none (user-pinned direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Validation: Default/expanded/closed/completed states, help, keyboard operation, reopening, light/dark, 320px width, no-battery copy, reduced effects and contrast fallbacks. Reuse only the existing brand, wallpaper, menu icon, and SF Symbol exports from the panel source; record their origin in the source. Ordinary system extension: preserve existing design-system files.
